Advanced Geospatial

UAV, LiDAR & GIS Mapping

High resolution aerial mapping and terrain intelligence tied to survey grade control. Faster data on large sites, without giving up the accountability of a licensed surveyor.

Overview

Survey Accuracy at Aerial Speed

Southline integrates UAV photogrammetry, LiDAR and GIS into the same workflow as our boundary and topographic surveys. Ground control and checkpoints are set by our crews, flights are planned to the accuracy your design requires, and every deliverable is reviewed by a Professional Land Surveyor.

The result is data your engineers can design on, your construction team can build from, and your lender or landowner can trust.

Capabilities

Geospatial Services

UAV Aerial Mapping

Drone based photogrammetry for rapid, high resolution mapping of large or hard to reach sites.

  • Orthomosaic imagery and site basemaps
  • Photogrammetric surfaces and contours
  • Construction progress and stockpile documentation
  • Solar, wind and pipeline site mapping
  • FAA Part 107 compliant operations

LiDAR & 3D Terrain Modeling

High density point clouds and terrain models for sites where vegetation, scale or accuracy rule out photogrammetry alone.

  • Digital terrain and surface models (DTM / DSM)
  • Classified point clouds and bare earth surfaces
  • Contours, cross sections and profiles
  • Cut/fill and earthwork volumetrics
  • Accuracy reporting to ASPRS standards

High Resolution Corridor Mapping

Purpose built mapping for pipelines, transmission lines, roadways and other linear infrastructure.

  • Route alignment and reconnaissance mapping
  • Right of way and easement mapping
  • Crossing inventories and utility conflict mapping
  • Long corridor data capture and alignment sheets

GIS & Remote Sensing Integration

Spatial data processing and GIS products that support planning, permitting, land acquisition and asset management.

  • GIS database and geodatabase development
  • Parcel, easement and landowner mapping
  • KMZ and web map exhibits for stakeholders
  • Data integration for engineering design (Civil 3D, ArcGIS, QGIS)
Technology

Equipment & Workflow

  • RTK / network GNSS receivers for control and checkpoints
  • Robotic total stations for high precision layout and verification
  • UAV platforms for photogrammetry and LiDAR acquisition
  • Photogrammetric and point cloud processing software
  • AutoCAD Civil 3D, ArcGIS Pro and QGIS deliverables
  • Coordinate systems, datums and geoid models documented on every deliverable

Process

How a Geospatial Project Runs

01

Define Accuracy

We confirm the accuracy class, coordinate system and deliverable formats your design team needs before anything is flown.

02

Set Control

Ground control and independent checkpoints are set with survey grade GNSS and tied to published control.

03

Acquire & Process

Flights are planned for the site, then imagery or LiDAR is processed, classified and checked against the control.

04

Deliver & Certify

Surfaces, orthos, point clouds and GIS files are delivered with an accuracy report and reviewed by the surveyor of record.
