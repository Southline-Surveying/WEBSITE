About Southline

A Licensed Surveyor You Can Actually Reach

Southline is a professional land surveying and mapping firm based in South Texas and working across Texas and the southern United States. Every project is run by a Registered Professional Land Surveyor who answers the phone, walks the site and signs the plat.

We serve homeowners who need a lot survey before a fence goes up, builders who need corners set this week, title companies and lenders who need a clean ALTA, and energy and infrastructure clients who need thousands of acres mapped to a defensible standard. Different scales, same standard of care.
