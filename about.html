About

Built on Accuracy. Run by a Licensed Surveyor.

Southline Surveying & Mapping is a Texas licensed surveying firm delivering boundary, title, construction and geospatial work with the care of a small firm and the capability of a much larger one.

Our Company

Small Firm Service, Large Project Capability

Southline was founded on a simple idea: clients deserve a licensed surveyor who is reachable, straightforward about scope and cost, and personally accountable for the work.

Based in South Texas, we serve homeowners, builders, developers, title companies, engineers and energy clients across Texas and the southern United States. Our work ranges from residential lot surveys to ALTA/NSPS title surveys, pipeline route surveys and aerial mapping of utility scale solar, wind and battery storage sites.

Every project is performed under the direct supervision of a Professional Land Surveyor with more than 15 years of field and office experience across residential, commercial, oil and gas and renewable energy work in multiple states.

Meet the Surveyor and Founder

Reachable, Experienced, Accountable

Obi Ubasineke, PLS, RPLS

Founder & Principal Surveyor

Southline is Obi and a team of experienced surveyors who answer the phone, review the records, stay reachable and work directly with the client from the first call to the sealed plat.

Obi founded Southline Surveying & Mapping to give property owners, builders and developers direct access to a licensed surveyor who is personally accountable for the work. A Professional Land Surveyor with more than 15 years of field and office experience, he has led survey and geospatial work on residential lots, commercial ALTA/NSPS surveys, oil and gas pipelines, and utility scale solar, wind, battery storage and data center projects across Texas and the southern United States. The team behind him brings the same standard to every job: clean CAD and GIS deliverables, documented coordinate systems and clear communication at every step.
