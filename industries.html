Who We Work With

Every client needs something different from a survey. We speak the language of each one, from a homeowner's fence line to an EPC contractor's site control network.

01

Residential & Homeowners

You are buying a home, building a fence, adding a pool or settling a question with a neighbor. You need a clear plat, corners you can find, and a surveyor who will explain what it means.

What we provide

  • Lot and improvement surveys for closings
  • Fence line and corner staking
  • Pool, addition and accessory building setbacks
  • Encroachment and easement questions

02

Commercial & Land Development

Developers, builders, attorneys and title companies need surveys that close deals and get plats recorded on schedule.

What we provide

  • ALTA/NSPS Land Title Surveys
  • Topographic and tree surveys for design
  • Subdivision plats, replats and lot line adjustments
  • Construction staking and as builts

Discuss Your Project

03

Solar, Wind & Battery Storage

Utility scale renewable projects cover thousands of acres and involve dozens of landowners, tight schedules and demanding lender requirements.

What we provide

  • ALTA/NSPS and boundary surveys for project sites
  • Aerial topographic mapping and LiDAR terrain models
  • Pile, road, inverter and substation layout
  • Gen tie and collector line route surveys
  • As built surveys and closeout exhibits

Discuss Your Project

04

Oil & Gas Pipelines

Midstream and upstream operators need right of way exhibits landowners will sign, alignments crews can build and as builts regulators will accept.

What we provide

  • Route reconnaissance and alignment surveys
  • Right of way, easement and workspace exhibits
  • Crossing and foreign line surveys
  • Centerline staking and as built surveys
  • Well pad, facility and lease boundary surveys

Discuss Your Project

05

Data Centers & Industrial

Mission critical facilities demand precise site control, complete existing conditions and disciplined coordination with multiple design teams.

What we provide

  • Site control networks
  • ALTA/NSPS and boundary surveys
  • Topographic and utility surveys
  • Construction layout and monitoring
  • UAV progress documentation

06

Engineers & Public Infrastructure

Civil engineers, municipalities and contractors need design grade base maps and right of way work that holds up in the plan review process.

What we provide

  • Design and topographic surveys
  • Right of way mapping and parcel exhibits
  • Control networks and benchmark circuits
  • Roadway, drainage and utility staking
  • GIS asset mapping
