Representative Projects

A cross section of the survey and geospatial work we perform across Texas and the surrounding areas. Project photos and case studies are added as clients approve them.

Boundary Survey · Bexar County, TX

Rural Acreage Boundary

Deed research, monument recovery and resolution of a conflicting adjoiner call on a multi tract ranch, with new corners set and a sealed plat and description.

Residential Lot Survey · San Antonio, TX

Improvement Survey for Closing

Lot corners recovered, house, fence and easements located, encroachment noted and plat delivered to the title company within the closing window.

ALTA/NSPS Survey · Central Texas

Commercial Title Survey

ALTA/NSPS survey with selected Table A items, Schedule B exception plotting and certification to buyer, lender and title underwriter.

Construction Staking · Comal County, TX

Commercial Site Layout

Site control, building corners, utilities, curb and grading stakes from approved civil plans, with form board verification and as builts.

Subdivision Platting · Guadalupe County, TX

Residential Replat

Replat of an existing tract into multiple lots, coordination with the platting authority, monumentation and recording.

Pipeline Route Survey · South Texas

Gathering System Right of Way

Alignment survey, easement exhibits and legal descriptions for landowner acquisition, followed by centerline staking and as built survey.

Solar Site Survey · Texas & Surrounding Areas

Utility Scale Solar Boundary & Topo

Boundary and ALTA/NSPS survey of multiple leased parcels with aerial topographic mapping to support civil design and pile layout.

LiDAR Terrain Model · Texas

Corridor Terrain Mapping

UAV LiDAR acquisition tied to survey control, classified bare earth surface, contours and cross sections delivered in Civil 3D.

UAV Orthomosaic · Texas

Construction Progress Mapping

Recurring drone flights producing georeferenced orthomosaics and volumetrics for a large earthwork site.

GIS Mapping · Multi County

Parcel & Easement Geodatabase

Landowner parcel, easement and route data compiled into a geodatabase and KMZ exhibits for land acquisition and stakeholder review.

Data Center Site Control · Texas & Surrounding Areas

Site Control & Topographic Survey

Primary control network, existing conditions survey and utility inventory to support design of a mission critical facility.

Wind & Storage Layout · Texas & Surrounding Areas

Boundary confirmation, access road and turbine pad staking, and battery storage site layout for a renewable energy project.
